About

A Voice Shaped by
Purpose & Passion

Solace Paul Edem is not just a teacher — she is a movement. A force that transforms the ordinary act of speaking into an extraordinary instrument of power, clarity, and soul.

With over 20 years of general teaching experience and more than 10 dedicated years in diction training, Solace has shaped the voices of children, adults, corporate executives, and communities across Nigeria. Her journey began with a simple, deep-seated conviction: that every person deserves to be heard, understood, and taken seriously — and that it all starts with how they speak.

As the Founder of Right Speech Concept, she has built an institution that stands at the intersection of linguistics, confidence, and character. Her approach is not merely academic — it is transformational. Students don't just learn to pronounce words differently; they leave her sessions walking differently, standing differently, and leading differently.

A seasoned MC with 5+ years of experience commanding audiences at award ceremonies, valedictory services, and celebrations, Solace brings the same warmth, authority, and energy she pours into coaching to every stage she graces.

Her belief is simple but profound: "The impact of teaching is indelible." A word spoken into the right life, at the right moment, with the right voice — changes everything.
